# T-023: Levels 9 and 10 ask questions with a gap of metres

## Bug
`LEVELS` rows 9 and 10 have `askedMin: 0`, so any pair with a non-zero asked gap is valid, however tiny. On the real data (a83bbe1), `validPairs` returns pairs such as Paris / Volzhsky, London / Accra and Rome / Chicago whose asked gap is 0.01 km (10 m).

Steps: in a test, call `validPairs(await getCitiesByDifficulty(10), 10)` and take `min(ns.km, ew.km)` from `getDistanceInfo` for each pair.

Counts of valid pairs by asked gap:
| Level | Valid pairs | Under 1 km | Under 10 km | Under 25 km |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | 7,064 | 55 | 464 | 1,236 |
| 10 | 7,325 | 71 | 675 | 1,826 |

Expected: a question the player can answer from a map. The reveal rounds to whole km (`formatDistance`), so a 0.01 km gap shows as "North by 0 km / 0 mi": the answer cannot be judged by the player or checked from the reveal. The brief's level 10 ("close on the asked axis") means close, not unresolvable.
Actual: about 1% of level-10 pairs round to 0 km on the reveal, and about 9% are under 10 km.

pm to confirm the floor (QA suggests at least 5 km on the asked axis, so the reveal never shows 0 km); dev then sets `askedMin` for levels 9 and 10 (and keeps the monotonic rule: askedMin may not rise with level, so levels 6 to 8 may need the same floor or the test's direction needs pm's ruling) and rechecks the 200-pair minimum.

## Acceptance
- A test shows no valid pair at any level has an asked-axis gap under 25 km (CEO Decision 11, brief criterion 12), in the same km the reveal shows, on the real data.
- The monotonic ladder test compares each level's effective asked minimum (the larger of the table value and the floor); levels tied at the floor must still be strictly harder on familiarity, the upper asked limit or the other-axis minimum.
- A test shows every level still has at least 200 valid pairs and the monotonic ladder test still passes.
- `npm test` and `npm run lint` pass.

## Plan
Per pm (brief criterion 12, Decision 11): one shared floor of 25 km on the asked axis at every level, not a per-level band; levels at the floor are tied on that dial.
Files: `web/src/services/levels.ts` (export `MIN_ASKED_KM = 25`; rows 9 and 10 get `askedMin: MIN_ASKED_KM` instead of 0), `web/src/services/quizService.ts` (`validPairs` uses `Math.max(askedMin, MIN_ASKED_KM)`, replacing the `asked > 0` rule, which the floor covers; the doc comment says so), tests in `levels.test.ts` and `quizService.test.ts`.
Approach: the floor is a constant used in one place in `validPairs`, so it holds at every level whatever the table says; the table's rows 9 and 10 show it explicitly. Effective minimum per level is `max(askedMin, 25)`: levels 1 to 8 are unchanged (their minimums are 50 or more), levels 9 and 10 become 25. Level 9 (pool 760, asked 25 to 150, other 3,000+) and level 10 (pool 1,000, asked 25 to 100, other 3,000+) stay strictly ordered: level 10 has a bigger pool and a lower upper limit. I measured the real data before the change: level 9 had 7,064 valid pairs with 1,236 under 25 km, level 10 had 7,325 with 1,826 under 25 km (QA's table), so about 5,800 and 5,500 remain; I re-run the counts on the data as it is when I build (after T-022's removals) and report them.
Existing test to update: `levels.test.ts` "leaves out a pair that shares a latitude exactly (Medan and Buenaventura), but not one a hair off" expects the pair nudged by 0.001 degrees to be valid, which the floor now forbids. I rewrite it: Medan and Buenaventura (exactly the same latitude) is still not a valid pair at level 10; the same pair nudged by 0.001 degrees (about 100 m) is now also rejected; the same pair nudged by 0.5 degrees of latitude (about 55 km, above the floor) stays valid. Renamed to say so.
Tests: (levels.test.ts) for every level, the smallest asked gap among its valid pairs (from `getDistanceInfo`, min of north-south and east-west km) is at least 25; the monotonic test uses the effective minimum `Math.max(askedMin, MIN_ASKED_KM)` and still requires strictly harder on at least one dial, so levels 9 and 10 cannot become identical; a second synthetic pair, cities 0.001 degrees of latitude apart (about 100 m) and 40 degrees of longitude apart, is not valid at level 10, while the same pair 0.3 degrees apart (about 33 km) is, which pins the 25 km edge on both sides; the 200-pair test stays. (quizService.test.ts) in the 50-quiz loop each question's asked gap is at least `MIN_ASKED_KM` as well as inside its table band.
Prove tests can fail on a copy outside the repo: set `MIN_ASKED_KM` to 1 (floor tests red); remove the `Math.max` and set rows 9 and 10 back to 0 (floor tests red); make level 10 identical to level 9 (monotonic test red); set the floor to 1,500 so a level drops under 200 pairs (200-pair test red).
Run `npm test`, `npm run lint`, `npx tsc -b`, `npm run build:web` in the foreground and read exit status.
